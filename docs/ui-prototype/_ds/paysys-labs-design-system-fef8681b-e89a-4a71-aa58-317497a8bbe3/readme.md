# Paysys Labs Design System

Paysys Labs is a fintech infrastructure and advisory firm founded in 2016, headquartered in the UAE with a corporate/sales office in the USA and its development center in Karachi–Islamabad–Lahore, Pakistan. It designs, builds, and operates payment-systems technology for banks, central banks, and payment schemes — real-time/instant payment systems (RAAST, AANI, PesaLink, BUNA), core payment switching, digital banking, card issuing/acquiring, 3-D Secure (ACS), fraud/AML systems (Tazama), and cross-border remittance — delivered as licensed products ("OpenSuite"), advisory engagements, and managed/hosted services. Clients and partners include National Bank of Pakistan, JS Bank, Allied Bank, Bank Alfalah group entities, Mashreq, Careem, EthSwitch, 1LINK, the Linux Foundation (Tazama), and central banks across 25+ countries.

## Content fundamentals

- **Product naming**: every product is "Open + Capability" — OpenConnect, OpenWallet, OpenDigital, OpenCMS, OpenAcquiring, OpenACS, OpenCredit, OpenRemit, OpenFRMS, OpenEngage, OpenPay — collectively "OpenSuite." Use this pattern for any new product name.
- **Taglines are three parallel beats**, `Adjective Noun.` × 3, period-separated, no conjunction: *"Faster Payments. Smarter Routing. Secure Integration."* (OpenConnect), *"Smarter Banking. Safer Experience. Seamless Access."* (OpenDigital). Reuse this pattern for new product headers.
- **Voice**: formal, third-person-leaning enterprise B2B ("Paysys Labs is a leading provider…", "We collaborate with financial institutions…"). Confident and benefit-first, not casual — no slang, minimal exclamation points, no emoji anywhere in the source material.
- **Repeated vocabulary**: "seamless," "secure," "real-time," "scalable," "enterprise-grade," "interoperable," "compliance." Section headers are benefit-oriented: "Key Benefits," "Core Features," "Who We Are?," "Three Strategic Pillars."
- **Numbers do a lot of talking**: "25+ Countries," "1B+ Transactions," "99.99% Uptime," "300+ People," "24/7." Always paired with a short label underneath, never a paragraph — see the `StatMetric` component.
- **FAQs in Q→A pairs** close out every product page ("What is Open Connect?" / "How does Open Connect ensure security?").
- **Company values** (verbatim, six of them, one line each): Merit, Value Creation, Integrity, Excellence, Inclusivity, Innovation — see `guidelines/brand/brand-values.card.html` for the full text.
- **Technical register**: the uploaded deck and product pages both speak fluently in payments-standard jargon without expanding it for a lay reader — ISO 20022 message codes (Pain.009, Pacs.008), scheme names (RAAST, 1LINK, RTGS), and acronyms (MSP, IPS, KYC/AML, JWT) are used as first-class vocabulary, not footnoted. Keep this register for technical/architecture content; simplify only for consumer-facing digital-banking copy (Open Digital reads slightly more plain-language than OpenConnect).

## Visual foundations

- **Color**: primary is a mid navy-blue (`--blue-600 #16659c`, confirmed live on paysyslabs.com), anchored by a near-black navy ink (`--navy-900 #0a2540`) used for header bands and hero/cover gradients (`linear-gradient(135deg, navy-900, blue-600)` — this exact diagonal navy→blue sweep is the single most repeated background treatment in Paysys' own collateral). Gold (`--gold-500 #f5a800`) is the sole accent — used only for the logo's angular mark and for highlighting individual keywords/numbers inside body copy, never as a large fill or a button color. A bright sky-cyan (`--sky-400 #33b5e5`) shows up in charts and small circular process icons. Neutrals are a cool slate ramp, not true gray. Semantic success/warning/danger were not present in any source and were derived to sit in the same cool, moderately-desaturated register as the brand blues (via oklch) rather than stock bright red/green/amber.
- **Typography**: display/headings use a rounded geometric sans with a bold-leaning weight — closest real inspection point is the "Company Profile" cover and section headers in the Company Profile PDF. Body copy is a plainer humanist sans. **Both are Google Fonts substitutions, not the real brand fonts** — see Caveats.
- **Backgrounds**: no photography, illustration, or texture anywhere in the source material except one stock cityscape photo on the Company Profile cover (not reusable — not brand IP) and a dotted-map graphic. The working pattern is flat color or the navy→blue diagonal gradient; nothing full-bleed-photographic should be assumed as "on brand" without more source material.
- **Cards**: white fill, 1px light-slate border, `--radius-lg` (16px) corners, soft low-contrast navy-tinted shadow (never a hard/dark shadow). The recurring "pillar" card pattern tops the card with a solid or gradient navy/blue header band holding the title in white, then plain white body text below — see `Card` with `headerTone`.
- **Icons**: simple single-color flat outline icons in a colored circle badge (ring + glyph), used for certifications and feature callouts. No icon font or sprite sheet was found in the source. See Iconography below.
- **Animation / interaction**: no motion design exists in any source (static PDF/PPTX/WordPress). Components here use only short (120–200ms) ease-standard transitions for hover/focus — restrained, not a documented brand behavior.
- **Hover/press**: not documented in any source; components here default to a one-step-darker fill on hover (e.g. `blue-600` → `blue-700`) and no press/shrink effect, which is a safe, common enterprise pattern rather than an observed brand rule.
- **Borders/shadow**: borders are 1–1.5px, light slate, never colored except on focus (blue ring, `--shadow-focus`). Shadows are soft and cool-toned (`rgba(10,37,64,…)`), scaling from barely-there (`sm`) to a noticeably lifted modal (`lg`).
- **Transparency/blur**: only seen once, as a dark navy scrim under the Company Profile cover photo — modeled as `rgba(10,37,64,0.55)` for the `Dialog` overlay backdrop. No frosted-glass/blur treatment anywhere in source.
- **Corner radii**: generous and consistent — ~16px on cards/pillars, smaller (~6–10px) on buttons/inputs/badges, full pill on chips/switches.
- **Layout**: source material is conventional top-nav marketing site + fixed-header slide decks; no unusual fixed/floating chrome observed.

## Iconography

No icon font, sprite sheet, or SVG set was included with the source material — the pptx has zero embedded assets, and the two icon usages visible on the live site (product-grid icons, certification badges) are simple flat single-color outline glyphs inside a circular ring, not attributable to a named icon library. Per the substitution rule, components/cards here link **Lucide** (`lucide-static`, MIT-licensed) from `unpkg` CDN — closest open match for stroke weight and rounded-cap style. ## Components

Standard set (no component-library source was provided, so this follows the design system skill's default primitive set, sized down slightly):

### Intentional additions (beyond the standard primitive set)

- **`Alert`** — Paysys' own copy leans heavily on compliance/security/status messaging (KYC/AML, PCI DSS, mandate validation); a dedicated inline banner earns its place beyond a bare `Toast`.
- **`StatMetric`** — direct match for the "1B+ / USD 3B+ / 99.99% / 24/7" big-number stat grids repeated throughout the Company Profile deck.
- **`ProcessSteps`** — direct match for the "Engage → Discover → Diagnose → Formulate → Recommend → Guide" consulting-process tracker and the company history/journey timeline in the same deck.
- **`FlowDiagram`** — the most source-grounded component in the system: a reusable version of the actor-lane, numbered-message swimlane diagrams that make up 100% of the uploaded "Mandate based Payments" deck.
