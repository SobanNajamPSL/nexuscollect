NexusCollect
Person-to-Government collection

A single platform through which a citizen or a business can settle anything they owe to government — and through which each agency can prove, to the rupee, what it collected and where that money went.

  • 01 One reference finds every bill. A vehicle number, a CNIC, a case number, a scanned QR — across every agency, in one list.
  • 02 Any channel can pay it. Bank app, branch counter, cheque, card, wallet, agent shop. The same pipeline behind all of them.
  • 03 Every rupee is attributed, and provably so. Split to the right revenue head, for the right agency, on an append-only ledger that can be re-checked at any time.

What you are about to see

Four portals, and one day’s money

Who uses it
  • Citizen
    Public. No account, no login. Find a bill, pay it, keep the receipt.
  • Agency
    One agency’s own position, head by head. Issue bills, request payment, hand money to treasury.
  • Operator
    The back office, across all agencies. Reconcile, investigate, approve, sweep, prove.
  • Field
    A counter or a shop. Cash, cheques, the till at end of shift.
